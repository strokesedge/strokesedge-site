01 — Course Overview

Black Desert Resort, two years in.

Black Desert Resort plays to a par of 71 at 7,421 yards, verified against both pgatour.com's current tournament page and the event's Wikipedia tournament record. Tom Weiskopf and Phil Smith built the course in 2023 through an ancient lava field in Ivins, Utah, at roughly 3,000 to 3,100 feet of elevation, land that plays shorter than its scorecard number in the thin desert air. The hole distribution is unusual for a Tour stop: 11 par 4s, four par 3s, and just three par 5s.

What decides the leaderboard: wide bentgrass fairways and flat, 7,000-square-foot greens make this one of the more gettable setups on Tour, Data Golf has it ranked the 9th-easiest course across its first two years, averaging -1.56 strokes to par per round. The two editions played so far were won at 23-under and 22-under. That means approach precision and putting separate the field more than raw distance does, while driving accuracy matters less for scoring than for simply staying out of the lava rock lining the corridors.
