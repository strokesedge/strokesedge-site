Formerly the ZOZO Championship, the Baycurrent Classic is only in its second year at Yokohama Country Club, a composite course with pine-lined zoysia fairways and bentgrass greens. Xander Schauffele won the first edition at 19-under, one clear of Max Greyserman. This page is built around one question: who has the approach and tee-to-green profile to repeat that kind of number on a layout with exactly one year of Tour data?

01 — Course Overview

Yokohama Country Club, year two.

Yokohama Country Club plays to a par of 71 at 7,322 yards, consistent across pgatour.com and independent course previews. The tournament uses a composite layout: 16 holes from the West Course plus the 17th and 18th from the East Course. The West Course was redesigned by Bill Coore and Ben Crenshaw in the mid-2010s, with bentgrass greens, Korai zoysia fairways, and Noshiba zoysia rough.

What decides the leaderboard: preview coverage describes wide corridors lined by pines and rough that is not especially penal, so approach play from the fairway is the clearest separator, with off-the-tee play and Data Golf's course-fit signals behind it. Schauffele won the 2025 edition at 19-under (265), and Matt McCarty set the course record with a 60 in the final round.

New name, new course changes, thin history
The event was the ZOZO Championship through 2024 at Narashino Country Club and became the Baycurrent Classic at Yokohama in 2025, so only one edition counts as course history. For 2026 the 18th was lengthened from 475 to 482 yards, tees were added on several holes, and trees right of the 18th fairway were removed. See the Yokohama Country Club Course Guide for the full breakdown.
