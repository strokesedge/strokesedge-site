01 — Course Overview

Port Royal, short and windy.

Port Royal plays to a par of 71 at 6,828 yards per pgatour.com's current tournament page, which differs slightly from the 6,842 yards Wikipedia lists for the course. Robert Trent Jones designed it, it opened in 1970, and a 2009 upgrade led by Roger Rulewich is the most recent major work we found. The 16th hole, with its tee and green on cliffs above the Atlantic, is the signature.

What decides the leaderboard: at under 6,900 yards, distance gives no clear scoring edge, so most approaches come from short and mid-iron range and the model weights approach and putting most heavily. The forecast window shows winds of 14-43 mph with gusts up to 60 mph, which adds a premium on accuracy and around-the-green play. Winning scores have ranged from 12-under to 24-under across seven editions.
