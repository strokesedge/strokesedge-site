RocketClassic.

Detroit Golf Club · Par 70 · 7,328 Yards · Jul 30–Aug 2, 2026

The 2026 Rocket Classic arrives at a freshly restored Detroit Golf Club. A $16.1 million restoration led by architect Tyler Rae converted two former par 5s into long par 4s, rebuilt every green toward Donald Ross's original shapes, and removed the pond guarding the par-5 14th. Par 70, 7,328 yards — down from the par-72 layout every prior Rocket Classic champion actually played. Tree-lined corridors and quick, contoured greens make this as much a putting contest as a ball-striking exam.

01 — Course Overview

Detroit Golf Club fingerprint.

Detroit Golf Club plays to a par 70 at 7,328 yards for the 2026 Rocket Classic (verified directly against pgatour.com's course-stats page — par and yardage confirmed hole-by-hole, not carried over from a prior year). The North Course is a Donald Ross design dating to 1899, and 2026 marks its most significant change since the tournament arrived in 2019.

What stats matter this week: SG: Approach and SG: Putting carry the two heaviest weights in the model, with SG: Around the Green close behind — a field that plays this low on quick, contoured greens rewards precise iron play and hot putting well ahead of raw distance.

Course change flag — 2026 restoration
Detroit Golf Club underwent a $16.1 million restoration ahead of this year's event (architect Tyler Rae, honoring Donald Ross's original design). The par-5 7th (505 yards) and par-5 17th (537 yards) were rebuilt as long par 4s, leaving only two par 5s on the course — the 4th (592 yards) and 14th (561 yards). Every green was rebuilt toward its original Ross shape, and the pond that guarded the par-5 14th was removed. This is functionally a new golf course. Course History and Course Experience are weighted at just 2% and 1% this week (down from a typical 4%/3%) because most of the field's past results here predate the current layout and carry less signal than usual.

Conditions this week: wind running around 10 mph with gusts up to 22 mph, temperatures ranging 62–89°F, and one day in the window carrying meaningful rain. Softer greens from that rain should favor players willing to be aggressive into the flag rather than those leaning on a firm-and-fast bounce.

Historical winning scores: Rocket Classic winners at Detroit Golf Club have finished between -18 and -26 since the event's 2019 debut (full table in Section 03 below) — but every one of those scores came on the old par-72 routing. With two strokes removed from par this year, this year's winning score won't be directly comparable to past years' — treat the historical range as context, not a target.

#2 — SG: Putting (17% weight)
Historically a putting contest on quick, contoured Ross greens where birdies pile up. Winner DNA gate: SG: Putting ≥ -0.10 — the model treats a clearly below-average putting week as too severe a handicap on greens this demanding.
#3 — SG: Around the Green (13% weight)
Missed greens around tight Ross green complexes require a sharp short game to save par. Paired with a Scrambling % gate (≥ 55%) and a Driving Accuracy gate (≥ -0.10, tree-lined corridors punish stray tee shots).
