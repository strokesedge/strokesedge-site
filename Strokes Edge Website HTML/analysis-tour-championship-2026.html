East Lake Golf Club · Par 70 · 7,440 yards · Aug 27–30, 2026 · No-cut, 30-player field

The FedEx Cup Playoffs close out at East Lake Golf Club, rebuilt by Andrew Green in 2023-24 and reshaped from the greens up. Thirty players, no cut, and for the first time no staggered start — every player begins the week at even par, straight stroke play, winner-take-all for the FedEx Cup. Approach play and putting dominate the model this week; the market's old "East Lake accuracy" read is stale evidence on a course that no longer plays that way.

01 — Course Overview

East Lake fingerprint.

East Lake Golf Club dates to 1904, has been the permanent home of the TOUR Championship since 2005, and just underwent its most significant renovation in decades — a 2023-24 Andrew Green restoration, unveiled at the 2024 TOUR Championship, that reshaped greens, bunkering, and strategic angles across the property along with a regrassing to a heat-tolerant Bermuda strain.

What stats matter this week: SG: Approach is the model's top factor at 17%, with SG: Off the Tee and SG: Putting close behind at 14% and 13%. Fairways were widened in the restoration, so driving accuracy carries almost no weight — precision into the new greens is what separates the field. Full numeric weights below.

#1 — SG: Approach (17% weight)
Long approach shots into rebuilt, more contoured greens make approach quality the top differentiator. Winner DNA gate: SG: Approach ≥ +0.05 — no plausible winner clears East Lake's reshaped greens without demonstrated current iron form.
#2 — SG: Off the Tee (14% weight)
7,440 yards demands strong tee-to-green ball-striking just to reach par-4 and par-5 greens in position — a par-4-heavy layout with only two par 5s on the card.
Driving Accuracy Fit — only 4%
Widened fairways from the 2023-24 restoration reduce the accuracy premium relative to older East Lake setups. The market's instinct to reward "tidy" East Lake specialists is stale evidence — the course simply doesn't punish a wayward tee shot the way it used to, as long as the approach that follows finds the green.
03 — Course History

Who has been here before.

East Lake has produced a run of approach-first champions — Schauffele in 2017, Cantlay in 2021, Scheffler in 2024 — all ranked near the top of the field in strokes gained on approach that week. Course History carries just 3% weight in the current model, down from a typical 5-6% combined, since the 2023-24 restoration makes pre-2023 East Lake history less representative of the course the field plays now.

Rory McIlroy — the clearest illustration of the discount
McIlroy is East Lake's only three-time winner (2016, 2019, 2022), but the model reads him as a FADE OUTRIGHT ONLY this week at -5.93% edge. His profile is solid across the board — nothing broken — but three East Lake titles were all won on a course that no longer exists in its 2023-24 form. The model weighs his current-season approach and putting numbers instead of the résumé, and this week that reads as a fade rather than a pedigree play.
