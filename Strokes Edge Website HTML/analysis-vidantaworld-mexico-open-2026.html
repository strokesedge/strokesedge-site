Vidanta Vallarta · Par 71 · 7,337 yards · Oct 29–Nov 1, 2026 · 120-player field

Vidanta Vallarta is a long, coastal paspalum course that was rerouted for this year, and the event moves to the fall for the first time. Brian Campbell won the 2025 edition at 20-under in a playoff. With six par 4s at 450 yards or more, this page is built to find the ball-strikers who can handle the length and the coastal breeze.

01 — Course Overview

Vidanta Vallarta, long and rerouted.

The tournament layout plays to a par of 71 at 7,337 yards. The resort's public scorecard is a par 73 at about 7,287 yards, so the two figures should not be mixed. Greg Norman designed the course, which has wall-to-wall paspalum, water in play on 12 holes, and coastal wind as its main defense.

What decides the leaderboard: the course was rerouted ahead of this event. It lost 99 yards (from 7,436 to 7,337), the front nine moved from par 35 to par 36, and four holes were renovated. The mix is four par 5s, five par 3s and nine par 4s, and six of the par 4s play 450 yards or more, led by the 496-yard 14th. At that length the model weights approach play and off-the-tee quality most heavily.

07 — FAQ

Quick answers.

What is par at Vidanta Vallarta for the 2026 VidantaWorld Mexico Open?
Par 71 at 7,337 yards for the tournament layout, per pgatour.com. The resort's public scorecard is a par 73, so do not mix the two figures.
Who is favored to win the 2026 VidantaWorld Mexico Open?
Wyndham Clark is the betting favorite at +1200. The StrokesEdge model's top-ranked player is Doug Ghim (+2000, L1 71.8), and its largest positive win edge among the top-ranked players belongs to Gordon Sargent (+6500, +2.71%). The model fades Clark outright.
When is the 2026 VidantaWorld Mexico Open and where is it played?
October 29 to November 1, 2026, at Vidanta Vallarta in Nuevo Nayarit, Mexico, with a $6 million purse and a 120-player field. It is the first time the event has been played in the fall.
Who won the last VidantaWorld Mexico Open?
Brian Campbell won the 2025 edition at 20-under, beating Aldrich Potgieter on the second playoff hole.
How low do scores go at Vidanta Vallarta?
Winning scores in the four events played here since 2022 were 17-under (Jon Rahm), 24-under (Tony Finau, the course record), 19-under (Jake Knapp) and 20-under (Brian Campbell, in a playoff), all on the pre-reroute layout.
Has Vidanta Vallarta changed since the last event?
Yes. The course was rerouted for 2026, cutting 99 yards from 7,436 to 7,337, moving to a par-36 front nine and renovating four holes, so past results carry little weight in the model.
