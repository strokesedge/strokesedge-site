WyndhamChampionship.

Sedgefield Country Club · Par 70 · 7,131 Yards · Aug 6–9, 2026

The 2026 Wyndham Championship closes the PGA Tour's regular season at Sedgefield Country Club, a Donald Ross parkland design that hasn't changed structurally since a 2007 restoration. Just 25 acres of fairway and thick Bermuda rough make this a positional course, not a power course — the 18th, a 507-yard closer, plays as a par 4 for the tournament even though it's a par 5 on the member card, which is where the tournament's par 70 comes from. Small, contoured Ross greens reward precise iron play and put a premium on wedge distances between 100 and 150 yards, an unusually large share of this course's approach shots.

Sedgefield fingerprint.

The course is a Donald Ross design last structurally restored in 2007 (architect Kris Spence, $3M, ten months), and nothing material has changed since.

What stats matter this week: SG: Approach carries the single heaviest weight in the model, with SG: Putting and the course-fit approach component close behind — a tight, tree-lined course with small greens rewards precision over power.
