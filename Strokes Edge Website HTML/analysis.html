02 — L1 Regression Weights

Renaissance Club requires a fundamentally different regression template than American parkland venues. Wide, minimal-rough fairways strip driving distance of its usual predictive weight, while firm, fast-running links turf elevates approach precision and scrambling. Links and European Tour pedigree function as a qualitative overlay on top of the standard SG-based score.
