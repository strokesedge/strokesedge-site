El Cardonal is a Tiger Woods design on the Baja coast with wide paspalum fairways and enormous greens. Ben Griffin won last year at 29-under, the course record. At 7,452 yards the model leans on approach play and off-the-tee quality, and this page is built to find the ball-strikers who can handle the length and the wind.

01 — Course Overview

El Cardonal, long and wide open.

The tournament layout plays to a par of 72 at 7,452 yards per pgatour.com's course stats (3,826 out, 3,626 in). Resort sources list 7,363 yards from the back tees, which is a different configuration. Tiger Woods designed the course through TGR Design, it opened in 2014, and it was the first Woods design to host a PGA Tour event. Fairways and greens are all paspalum.

What decides the leaderboard: the fairways are wide and the greens are large, averaging about 8,300 square feet, so the main trouble is the desert arroyos, which can run 100 yards or longer. The wind often blows from the north and can swing in off the ocean. The hole mix is four par 5s, four par 3s and ten par 4s, with the 601-yard 6th and 585-yard 18th the longest holes. At this length the model weights approach play and off-the-tee quality most heavily.
