01 — Course Overview

Fazio Canyons, long and brand new.

The tournament layout plays to a par of 72 at 7,451 yards per pgatour.com. Fazio Canyons is a Tom Fazio design at Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa in Austin, and this is the first PGA Tour event held on it. The purse is $6 million, the winner takes $1.08 million and 500 FedExCup Fall points, and the field is 120 players.

What decides the leaderboard: nobody knows yet. With no Tour data on the course, we have not invented a hole-by-hole story. What the scorecard does say is long: plenty of approach shots from 170 yards and out, so the model weights approach play and off-the-tee quality most heavily. The Tour set-up (rough height, green speed, hole locations) will be clearer after the first round.
