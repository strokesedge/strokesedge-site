01 — Course Overview

Seaside, restored for 2026.

The RSM Classic is played over the Seaside and Plantation courses at Sea Island Golf Club. Seaside was restored in 2026 by Love Golf Design and reopened on October 19. The work added about 152 yards (the course now plays 7,157 yards at par 70, against 7,005 before), rebuilt the greens, restored the bunkers and installed new paspalum tees, fairways and rough. The purse is $7.4 million and Sami Valimaki is the defending champion.

What decides the leaderboard: nobody can say for certain, because the renovated Seaside has no Tour scoring history. Rebuilt greens and a longer layout should raise the value of approach play and putting, and the model leans on those skills and on current form. Plantation was not part of the renovation, but Data Golf blends both courses in its course-fit and history numbers, so we treat those as partly pre-renovation as well.

Winner DNA gates this week
A player must clear SG: Approach of -0.05 or better and SG: Putting of -0.10 or better, plus floors for greens in regulation and double-bogey avoidance that most of the field clears, to qualify for outright and longshot tiers. A player with a missing gate stat is not assumed to pass. Players who miss a gate stay eligible only for finish markets.
