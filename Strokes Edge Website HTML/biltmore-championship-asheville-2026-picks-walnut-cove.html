  • SG: Approach >= -0.05. A long, approach-demanding course should filter out plausible winners with below-average iron play on Data Golf's scale.
  • Driving Distance Fit >= -0.15. At 7,249 yards, players notably shorter than tour average are unlikely realistic contenders.

Weather

Wind running 4-11 mph across the tournament window, gusts up to 18 mph. Temperatures 63-90F. 3 day(s) in the window carrying meaningful rain.

Wind stays manageable most days, 4 to 11 mph with gusts capping around 18, so this isn't a links-style crapshoot where variance swallows the leaderboard. The bigger factor is rain. Three days of meaningful precipitation in the window means softer greens, less roll, and a course that plays even longer than the yardage suggests. That combination shifts the emphasis further toward approach play and off-the-tee distance, since firm-and-fast shotmaking tricks lose value when the ball just stops.
