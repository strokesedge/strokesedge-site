BMW Championship 2026 Recap: Wyndham Clark Wins at Bellerive, Cantlay's Runner-Up Cashes the Model's Only Ticket, and East Lake Is Next

Bellerive Country Club · St. Louis, MO · Final Sunday 2026-08-23

Published Aug 24, 2026

The 2026 BMW Championship at Bellerive Country Club is in the books. Wyndham Clark closed it out in style, three straight birdies over the final three holes to hold off Patrick Cantlay by three shots. It was Clark’s third win of the season and sixth career PGA TOUR title. Here’s how the week played out, how StrokesEdge’s picks held up against it, and where the FedEx Cup standings sit heading into the season-ending TOUR Championship.

01, Final Leaderboard

Clark carded a final-round even-par 70 and won it with the putter down the stretch. Cantlay’s runner-up was the week’s biggest storyline outside the winner’s circle, a finish that also happened to be the model’s single winning ticket and one that reshaped the FedEx Cup bubble (more on that below).

02, How the Model’s Picks Actually Played

The model’s headline call this week was a fade on Scottie Scheffler outright, “FADE OUTRIGHT ONLY, Top 10/20 still viable”, built on a real tension in his profile: field-best approach play (100th percentile, +1.265 SG) and elite tee-to-green numbers producing the top L1 score (82.7), but a -6.67% edge suggesting the market had already priced in that dominance. Scheffler finished T12, outside both the win and a Top 10 cash, which is exactly the outcome the fade logic was built to avoid overpaying for.

Worth flagging plainly: the tracker carried a straight outright Winner bet on Scheffler anyway ($25 at +375), which lost. That’s a direct deviation from the model’s own recommendation that week, not a model miss. The model’s preferred angle, a Top 20 parlay leg, also didn’t cash, but that was due to the parlay’s other legs, not Scheffler himself (he comfortably made Top 20).

The three graded E/W Winner plays all missed. Xander Schauffele (+1800 model / +1600 E/W ticket, rank #2, +4.21% edge) finished T26, well outside win or place range. Hideki Matsuyama (+3000, rank #3, +1.73% edge) finished T28. Cameron Young (+2000, rank #4, a razor-thin +0.02% edge the model itself flagged as barely a value play) finished T37, the week’s worst outcome among the graded names. That last one was a case where the model’s own numbers already signaled there wasn’t much conviction behind the pick.

Longshot/Value: Adam Scott (+6000 model / +5500 ticket, rank #12, +1.15% edge) finished T16, no outright cash but a live, credible week for a longshot ticket. Michael Brennan, the model’s other Longshot/Value name (+6000, rank #7, +1.75% edge, anchored by a 96th percentile driving distance fit), wasn’t logged as a bet this week at all. The free Substack pick and the actual tracked wager list diverge sometimes, which is normal and not an error.

Top 10/Top 20 was the week’s bright spot. Patrick Cantlay (Top 20 ticket, rank #6, +0.56% edge, built on a 96th percentile approach game) delivered the tracker’s only win of the week with a runner-up finish, about as clean a validation of the model’s approach-driven thesis as this tier gets. Ludvig Åberg (Top 10, rank #5, field-best 100th percentile off-the-tee) finished T16, a near-miss just outside the cash line. Sam Burns (Top 10, rank #8, 98th percentile approach undercut by 34th percentile driving) finished T28. Si Woo Kim (Top 20, rank #9) finished T33. Kurt Kitayama (Top 20, rank #10) finished T26, missing by six spots and also one of the bigger storylines heading into East Lake (see below).

Matchups (free picks, not logged as bets this week): Matsuyama over Burns landed as a dead-even push, both finishing tied at T28. Kim over Fitzpatrick went the other way; Fitzpatrick finished five spots better (T28 vs. T33), so the model’s lean was wrong there.

03, FedEx Cup Standings After BMW

Scottie Scheffler remains atop the standings heading into the TOUR Championship, but the gap has tightened since St. Jude.

Other tracked names: Rory McIlroy 11th (2,095.449), Xander Schauffele 12th (2,024.897), Hideki Matsuyama 16th (1,637.268), Patrick Cantlay 19th (1,529.208), Adam Scott 28th (1,383.051).

Cantlay’s runner-up was the single biggest mover of the week. He jumped from 43rd all the way to 19th, joined by Gary Woodland (now 18th) and Robert MacIntyre (now 25th) as the week’s other major climbers into the top 30 field for East Lake. Their arrival pushed Rickie Fowler, Bud Cauley, and Kurt Kitayama out. Kitayama’s own near-miss Top 20 ticket this week (T26, six spots short) was part of the story that cost him his spot in the season finale.

Only the top 30 in points advance to the TOUR Championship, and that field is now locked.

04, Looking Ahead: TOUR Championship

The FedEx Cup Playoffs close out at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta for the TOUR Championship, August 27-30. Par 70, 7,440 yards, 30-man field with no cut and no staggered start this year. Every player begins at even par, straight stroke play, winner-take-all for the FedEx Cup. East Lake also underwent a major Andrew Green restoration in 2023-24, its first real look under tournament pressure.
