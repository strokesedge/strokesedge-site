El Cardonal at Diamante · Cabo San Lucas, Mexico · Par 72 · 7,452 tournament yards · Host: World Wide Technology Championship (Nov 5-8)

El Cardonal at Diamante is a Tiger Woods design on the Baja coast, and Ben Griffin set the course record here last year at 29-under 259. With wide paspalum fairways, enormous greens and 7,452 yards, this is an approach and off-the-tee course where the desert arroyos and the wind are the main defense.

01 — Course Fingerprint

A wide, long desert course where the arroyos bite.

The tournament layout plays to a par of 72 at 7,452 yards (3,826 out, 3,626 in), per pgatour.com's course stats. Resort sources list 7,363 yards from the back tees with a 75.9 rating and 140 slope, which is a different configuration, so resort figures do not apply directly to this week. Tiger Woods designed the course through TGR Design, it opened in 2014, and it was the first Woods design to host a PGA Tour event. Fairways, rough and greens are all paspalum.

The fairways are wide and the greens are large, averaging about 8,300 square feet and mostly canted on a diagonal. The main hazards are desert arroyos, sandy gullies that can run 100 yards or longer, plus bunkers and native desert vegetation. The wind often blows from the north and can swing in off the ocean. The hole mix is four par 5s (1, 6, 14, 18), four par 3s (2, 9, 11, 16) and ten par 4s. The 474-yard 8th and the 554-yard 14th, which climbs about 80 feet from tee to green, are two of the tougher tests. The model treats El Cardonal as an approach and off-the-tee course, with putting and short-game skill behind them.

Small sample: history is a weak signal
The event moved here in 2023, so only three editions have been played at El Cardonal, and the model gives course history no weight this week. The weather window shows winds of 8-17 mph with gusts up to 36 and one day of meaningful rain, but forecasts move before Thursday.

04 — Betting Angles

How to bet a long, wide-open layout.

01
Lead with iron play and tee-ball quality
Approach and off-the-tee are the two highest-weighted categories in the model this week. Players who hit it far enough to shorten the long holes and then hit quality irons get the most birdie looks.
02
Do not overpay for course history
Three editions is a small sample, and the defending champion's price already reflects his record 29-under win. The model likes Ben Griffin's iron play but fades his outright price.
03
Watch the wind
Gusts up to 36 mph are in the forecast window and the wind can swing off the ocean. Check conditions before Thursday, because wind exposes shaky iron play.
