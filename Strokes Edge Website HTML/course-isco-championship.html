01 — Course Fingerprint

TPC Louisiana's structural profile.

Pete Dye designed TPC Louisiana with Steve Elkington and Kelly Gibson. The course rewards aggressive scoring from tee to green. Wide fairways invite distance-forward strategies. Four par 5s all reachable in two for the field leaders. Five par 4s under 403 yards. The architecture is built for birdies.

The defense comes from precision on approach. Over 100 bunkers placed to penalize shots that miss the correct quadrant. Water on seven holes including a peninsula 18th that has ended tournament bids on Sunday. Soft, sea-level conditions add carry value and make birdie production easier than at inland courses.

Model classification
TPC Louisiana is a birdie-production course with a precision filter on approach and par-5 conversion. The player profile that wins: distance to convert scoring holes in two (or create short irons for approach), elite SG: Approach to manufacture birdies, and par-5 mastery to build a scoring separation that the field cannot replicate. Grinders without birdie ceiling do not win here. Bombers without approach precision leak strokes in bunkers.
02 — Predictive Stats

What the numbers actually predict.

Correlation with finishing position at TPC Louisiana based on winner and top-10 finisher data, cross-referenced with ISCO Championship scoring trends.

SG: Approach the Green
#1 Predictor
Approach play generates birdie chances on short par 4s and reachable par 5s. Winners correlate strongly with top-tier approach numbers. The soft greens reduce penalty for misses but reward precision heavily for scoring upside.
SG: Par 5 / Birdie Conversion
#2 Predictor
All four par 5s reachable. Winning margins are often built on eagle and birdie conversion on these holes. Players who gain multiple strokes per round on par 5s carry a structural scoring advantage.
Driving Distance
#3 Predictor
Sea-level conditions and soft fairways amplify the distance premium. Bombers convert short par 4s into wedge-in birdie opportunities the field cannot match. Distance carries above-normal Tour-wide predictive value here.
