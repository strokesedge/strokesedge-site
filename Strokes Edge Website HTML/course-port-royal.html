Port RoyalGolf Course.

Southampton, Bermuda · Par 71 · 6,828 tournament yards · Host: Butterfield Bermuda Championship (Oct 22-25)

Port Royal has hosted the Bermuda Championship every year since 2019, and at 6,828 yards it is a short test by Tour standards. Adam Schenk won last year at 12-under (272), one shot clear of Chandler Phillips, after Rafael Campos won at 19-under in 2024. With distance close to irrelevant, this is an approach, putting and wind-management course, and the forecast calls for plenty of wind.

01 — Course Fingerprint

A short coastal course where wind sets the number.

Port Royal plays to a par of 71 at 6,828 yards, per pgatour.com's current tournament page. Wikipedia lists the course at 6,842 yards, so the tournament set-up is slightly shorter than the general figure. Robert Trent Jones designed the course, which opened in 1970 and was upgraded in 2009 in a project led by Roger Rulewich, a member of the original design team. The 16th hole, with its tee and green on cliffs above the Atlantic, is the best known on the property.

At under 6,900 yards the course gives long hitters no clear scoring edge, so most approach shots come from short and mid-iron range. The model treats Port Royal as an approach and putting course, with around-the-green play and wind management behind them, and gives driving distance almost no weight.

Wind is the big variable this week
The forecast window shows winds of 14-43 mph with gusts up to 60 mph and several days with meaningful rain. Forecasts at that range move, so check conditions before you lock in a price. Past winning scores here have ranged from 12-under to 24-under, which is a wide range for a short course and mostly a weather story.

04 — Betting Angles

How to bet a short course in the wind.

01
Lead with iron play
Approach is the highest-weighted category in the model this week. At this length, players with strong iron numbers get the most birdie looks and the cleanest route to a good number.
02
Do not pay for distance
Driving distance is nearly neutral at 6,828 yards. A bomber's price should not carry a premium here, which makes long hitters more likely to be overbet.
03
Respect the wind and the short game
Gusts to 60 mph are in the forecast window. Wind leaves more approaches off the green, which raises the value of around-the-green skill and scrambling, and it favors steady ball-strikers over players who rely on touch.
04
The field is thin, so edges sit further down the board
The model finds its largest positive win edges in the +3300 to +8000 range, and the betting favorite is priced past the model's number. Check the analysis page for the full tiered card.
