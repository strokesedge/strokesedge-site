01 — Course Fingerprint

What Makes Shinnecock Different

Shinnecock sits on a hillside in the Hamptons, exposed on all sides to Long Island wind. The William Flynn design uses a triangular routing so that almost no two consecutive holes play in the same wind direction. That is intentional. It means a player cannot find a rhythm adjusting to one direction the way they might on an out-and-back links layout. Every hole is its own problem.

The par-4 distribution is unusual and matters for how you build the regression. There are six par 4s measuring 490 yards or longer, but also four measuring under 415 yards. That bimodal spread means mid-iron approach play is less separating than at a conventional US Open setup, where most par 4s cluster between 420 and 470 yards. The long holes get attacked with hybrids and long irons, the short ones with wedges. It is a completely different skill set than the one SG:APP normally captures.

The greens are the other variable. Crowned, fast, and fiercely position-dependent. Balls on the wrong side of a pin can be unplayable. The 2018 setup saw balls rolling off greens with players watching. In conditions like that, around-the-green scrambling and lag putting become the primary separation stats.

Model Note — L1 Regression Adjustment
SG:APP weight is reduced below standard for Shinnecock due to the bimodal par-4 distribution. SG:ARG and SG:PUTT are elevated. This is one of the few courses where the standard course regression template requires meaningful adjustment before running.
02 — Regression Weights

L1 Stat Model

How the StrokesEdge model weights each strokes gained category for Shinnecock Hills. Weights deviate meaningfully from the standard PGA Tour template given the course's unique demands.

SG: Around the Green (ARG) #1 PREDICTOR · 30%
Crowned greens at 11+ on the Stimp make scrambling and chipping precision the primary separator. Balls miss greens here even from the fairway. What you do from 20 yards is what matters.
SG: Putting (PUTT) ELEVATED · 25%
Particularly approach putting (lag) on fast surfaces. Three-putt avoidance from distance is a huge score differential on greens this quick. Players who bail wide of pins need elite pace control.
SG: Approach the Green (APP) REDUCED · 25%
Still important but weighted below standard due to the bimodal par-4 distribution. Long-iron and wedge approaches dominate, not the 150–180 yard mid-iron band where SG:APP gains its largest sample.
SG: Off the Tee (OTT) BELOW STANDARD · 20%
Wide fairways mean raw distance is not the weapon it is at many major setups. Positioning matters more than carry distance. Driving accuracy becomes more relevant than SG:OTT pure value.

Pattern Recognition
Four of five winners finished at par or worse. No one runs away from the field at Shinnecock — the margins are tight and the variance is high. That matters for market positioning: outright prices on the favorites are compressed by name recognition more than the data actually supports. The 2018 runner-up Tommy Fleetwood shot a final-round 63 and still lost. Finish markets carry more value here than outright volume.
06 — Betting Angles

What the Model Targets

01
Fade raw SG:APP leaders who lack ARG
Players ranked top-10 in SG:APP but below average around the green tend to disappear at Shinnecock. The approach gains do not transfer to scoring when the greens punish even well-struck irons. Target the ARG rank first, not APP.
02
Wind-tested ball-strikers outperform
Players with links or coastal tournament experience in high-wind conditions historically outperform their SG numbers at Shinnecock. It is a qualitative overlay on the model output, not a standalone pick driver.
03
Finish markets over outright volume
The historical winner pattern (tight margins, over-par winning scores) signals high variance. Top 10 and Top 20 finish markets carry better expected value than outright exposure at most price points. Small outright with larger finish market position is the recommended structure.
04
Thursday tee time is a live variable
With 30+ mph gusts forecast for R1, late starters could be playing a materially different test than early morning groups. Check the tee sheet before finalizing any picks. Draw group matters more this week than almost any other event on the calendar.
05
Avoid big-number histories at Shinnecock
Some players mentally unravel at courses where a bad hole can cascade quickly. If a player's 2018 scorecard had a Saturday blowup or a round over 78, treat that as soft negative data. It is not disqualifying but it informs the L3 screen.
