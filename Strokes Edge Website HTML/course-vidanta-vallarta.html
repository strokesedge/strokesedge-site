01 — Course Fingerprint

A long coastal course that just changed shape.

The tournament layout plays to a par of 71 at 7,337 yards, per pgatour.com's current tournament page. The resort's public scorecard is a par 73 at roughly 7,287 yards, which is a different configuration, so figures from resort listings do not apply to this week. Greg Norman designed the course, and the fairways and greens are wall-to-wall paspalum.

The 2026 reroute cut 99 yards from the previous 7,436, moved the front nine from par 35 to par 36, and renovated four holes, including a lengthened 4th that now turns slightly right to left. The hole mix is four par 5s, five par 3s and nine par 4s, and six of the par 4s play 450 yards or more, led by the 496-yard 14th. The model treats Vidanta Vallarta as an approach and off-the-tee course, with putting and short-game skill behind them.

Material course change: history is a weak signal
Because the course was rerouted and four holes were renovated, results from earlier editions do not map cleanly onto this week's layout. The model gives course history 2% and course experience 1%, and this is the first time the event has been played in the fall. The weather window shows winds of 8-9 mph with gusts up to 20, heavy rain on Wednesday and roughly a 50% chance Thursday, so expect a softer course early and a low-scoring week, but forecasts move.

03 — Recent Results

Recent winners, on a changed course.

04 — Betting Angles

How to bet a long coastal layout.

01
Lead with iron play and tee-ball quality
Approach and off-the-tee are the two highest-weighted categories in the model this week. Players who hit it far enough to shorten the long par 4s and then hit quality irons get the most birdie looks.
02
Do not lean on old course history
The reroute and four renovated holes mean past finishes here are a weak guide. A player's price should not carry a premium for earlier results at a layout that no longer exists in the same form.
03
Watch the course-fit gap
Several well-priced names grade out well on raw ball-striking but poorly on Data Golf's course-fit component, which is why the model fades the favorite outright while keeping finish markets live.
04
The edges sit further down the board
The model's largest positive win edges are in the +6500 to +15000 range, and the top of the market is priced past the model's number. Check the analysis page for the full tiered card.
