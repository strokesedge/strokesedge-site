Course Guide · 2026 Baycurrent Classic

YokohamaCountry Club.

Yokohama, Kanagawa, Japan · Par 71 · 7,322 tournament yards · Host: Baycurrent Classic (Oct 8-11)

The Baycurrent Classic, formerly the ZOZO Championship, is only in its second year at Yokohama Country Club after years at Narashino. Xander Schauffele won the first edition here in 2025 at 19-under (265), one shot clear of Max Greyserman, with a closing 64. Pine-lined zoysia fairways, bentgrass greens, and a Coore and Crenshaw redesign make this a ball-striker's course where approach play is the clearest separator.

01 — Course Fingerprint

A composite layout built on iron play.

The tournament uses a composite layout of 16 holes from Yokohama's West Course plus the 17th and 18th holes from the East Course, playing to a par of 71 at 7,322 yards. Both figures are consistent across pgatour.com and independent course previews. The West Course was redesigned by Bill Coore and Ben Crenshaw in the mid-2010s.

Greens are bentgrass, fairways are Korai zoysia, and the rough is Noshiba zoysia. Preview coverage describes wide corridors bordered by dense pines and rough that is not especially penal, so the course rewards controlled approach play from the fairway more than it punishes a miss. The model treats Yokohama as an approach-first, ball-striking course, with off-the-tee play and course-fit components behind it.

2026 course changes to know about
The 18th hole was lengthened from 475 to 482 yards, fairway lines were adjusted on holes 2 and 15, new tees were added on holes 5, 8, 9, 10, 12 and 18, and trees right of the 18th fairway were removed to expose the penalty area. These are modest changes rather than a redesign, but with only one prior edition at this venue, the model keeps course history and experience weights small.

03 — Results at Yokohama

One edition, a one-shot finish.

Schauffele won in 2025 at 19-under (265) by one stroke over Greyserman, closing with a 64 for his first win since the 2024 Open Championship. Matt McCarty shot the course record 60 in the final round that week. Before 2025 the event was played at Narashino Country Club, so those results say little about this layout.

04 — Betting Angles

How to bet a composite course with one year of data.

01
Lead with approach play
Approach is the highest-weighted category in the model this week and the first factor in outside course previews. Players with elite iron play from the fairway have the cleanest route to a good number.
02
Do not pay for course history
One prior edition is a very small sample, and the 2026 tee and fairway changes shift the layout slightly. Current form and course-fit signals carry more information than a single finish.
03
Watch the wind
The forecast shows winds of 4-11 mph with gusts up to 29 mph. Gusts that spike unpredictably favor stable ball-strikers over players who rely on touch.
04
Short fields punish thin edges
With only 72 players and a limited-field FedExCup Fall slot, favorites are priced tightly. The model's value this week sits in the +2000 to +8000 range rather than at the top of the board.
