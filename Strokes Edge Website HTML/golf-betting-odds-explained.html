Betting basics

Golf betting odds explained.

Reading a price is the first skill in golf betting. Here is how American, decimal and fractional odds work, and how to turn any of them into a probability.

What +1500 and -150 mean

American odds show profit on a winning 100 unit stake. A plus number is the profit on 100 staked, so a golfer at +1500 pays 1500 profit on a 100 bet, a return of 1600 with your stake. A minus number is how much you must stake to win 100, so -150 means staking 150 to win 100. Almost every golf outright is a plus number, because even the favorite is far less likely to win than not.

How to find implied probability

For a plus price, divide 100 by the price plus 100. For +1500 that is 100 / 1600, or 6.25%. For a minus price, divide the price by the price plus 100. For -150 that is 150 / 250, or 60%. Decimal odds are simpler: divide 1 by the decimal price (1 / 16.00 is 6.25%). You can check any price with the golf odds calculator.

Why the probabilities add up to more than 100%

If you convert every golfer's outright price to a probability and add them, the total is well above 100%. The excess is the sportsbook's margin, often called the overround, and it is the price of placing the bet. Longshots tend to carry a lot of it, because a book shades every long price in its own favor. That is why a model needs a real edge, not just a probability that looks about right.

Where value comes from

A bet has value when your estimated chance of winning is higher than the implied probability in the price. If a model gives a golfer a 3.0% chance to win and the price is +4000 (2.4% implied), the bet has a positive edge on paper. If the model says 2.0%, the same price is a pass. Value is always relative to the price, never to how good the golfer is. FAQ

What does +1500 mean in golf betting?

A golfer at +1500 pays 1500 profit on a 100 bet, a total return of 1600 including the stake. The implied win probability is about 6.25%.

How do I convert American odds to decimal?

For a plus price, divide by 100 and add 1, so +1500 is 16.00. For a minus price, divide 100 by the price and add 1, so -150 is about 1.67.

Why do golf odds add up to more than 100%?

The extra is the sportsbook's margin. Every golfer's price is shaded slightly, and long prices carry the most, so the implied probabilities across the field total well above 100%.
