How to Bet on Golf.

A plain-English walkthrough for first-time golf bettors, from picking a sportsbook to tracking your results.

Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

Step 1: Pick a licensed sportsbook

Use a sportsbook that is legal and licensed where you live, and check the age rule. Most US states require you to be 21. Compare a few books, because golf prices differ.

Step 2: Learn the odds

American odds show profit on a $100 stake. A +3000 price means $3,000 profit on $100, which implies about a 3.2 percent chance. Our odds guide has a quick conversion table.

Step 3: Choose a market

New bettors usually do better with top 10, top 20 and matchup markets than with outrights. Hits come more often, which teaches you the process faster.

Step 4: Research the course

Read the course guide and check which stats the course rewards. Every StrokesEdge analysis starts with that. Then check the weather and the field.

Step 5: Size your bet

Decide on a unit before the week starts. Keep it small and the same for every bet of the same type.

Step 6: Track everything

Write down the player, market, odds, stake and result. The StrokesEdge results page shows what an honest record looks like, wins and losses together.

FAQ

How do I start betting on golf?

Choose a licensed sportsbook where you live, learn how odds and the main markets work, set a small fixed unit, and record every bet. Start with finish-position or matchup markets before outrights.

What is an each-way bet in golf?

An each-way bet is two equal bets: one on the player to win and one on the player to place. The place portion pays at a fraction of the win odds. Terms vary by sportsbook.

How much should I bet on golf?

Many bettors use a fixed unit of about one to two percent of their bankroll. Smaller units suit longshots. Only bet money you can afford to lose.

Do you have to be 21 to bet on golf?

In most US states the legal age for sports betting is 21. Check the rules where you live.
