What is inside the workbook

  • Cover. The top picks at a glance, the fades and the matchups, with a glossary of every stat.
  • Dashboard. The top players by model score in one chart.
  • Picks Card. Every pick tier with odds, model rank, edge, the reasoning and whether the player clears the course thresholds.
  • Value Screen. Players who clear the thresholds, sorted by the gap between the model's probability and the market's.
  • Model Rankings. The full field, one row per player, with every input to the score and the DraftKings salary beside the name.
  • Watch List. Players outside the regression, such as LIV or DP World players with too little data, with the reason for each.
  • Model Weights. The weight on each stat for that course, where the data comes from and the thresholds used.

The workbook is a research tool. It shows how the model scores every player and where it sees value. It does not tell you what to bet, and no pick is guaranteed. You must be 21 or older to bet.

FAQ

What is in the StrokesEdge workbook?

Seven sheets: Cover, Dashboard, Picks Card, Value Screen, Model Rankings, Watch List and Model Weights. Together they show the model's score for every player in the field, the picks and fades, and the weights used for that course.

What is the difference between the $7 workbook and the $21 membership?

The $7 workbook covers one tournament. The $21 membership includes the workbook for every main event while you are a member. Buying single workbooks costs more once you want more than three tournaments in a month.

Are the weekly picks free?

Yes. The weekly picks article is free by email before each PGA Tour event. The workbook adds the full-field rankings and the data behind the picks.

Who is the workbook for?

People who want to see how a course-specific strokes gained model scores every player, not just the top picks. It is for research and entertainment, not financial advice.
