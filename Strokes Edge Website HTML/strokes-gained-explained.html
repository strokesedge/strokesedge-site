Strokes Gained Explained.

The statistic behind modern golf analysis, what each category measures, and how to turn it into betting decisions.

Updated November 2026 · 7 min read

What strokes gained is

Strokes gained compares each shot to the average result from the same spot. If the average player takes 2.8 strokes to hole out from a certain position and a player does it in 2, they gained 0.8. Add those up across a round and you can see where a player is winning or losing strokes.

The idea was popularized by Columbia professor Mark Broadie and became an official PGA Tour statistic in 2011. It replaced older stats like greens in regulation and putts per round, which hid too much context.

The four categories

CategoryWhat it measures
Off the teeDriving: distance and accuracy on par 4s and par 5s.
ApproachIron and long shots into the green from outside the short game area.
Around the greenChipping, pitching and bunker play near the green.
PuttingEverything on the green.

Tee-to-green is the sum of the first three. Total is all four. Putting is the noisiest, so a hot putting week is a weaker signal than a strong ball-striking week.

How to use it for betting

  1. Pull each player's strokes gained by category over a meaningful window, not one hot event.
  2. Decide which categories the course rewards.
  3. Score players on those categories, then compare the score to the odds.

That last step is the whole game. A player with a great profile at a short price is not a bet. A player with a good profile at a long price might be.

Cautions

  • Strokes gained numbers differ by provider. Pick one source and stay consistent.
  • Small samples mislead. Treat a player with few recent rounds as unknown, not bad.
  • Strokes gained describes the past. It does not know about injuries or equipment changes.

FAQ

What does strokes gained mean in golf?

Strokes gained measures how many strokes a player gains or loses on a shot or category compared with the average of a reference field. A positive number means better than average.

Which strokes gained stat is most important for betting?

It depends on the course. Approach is the most common predictor of finishing position, but off the tee, around the green and putting each take the lead at certain venues.

Is strokes gained the same across data providers?

No. Providers use different baselines, fields and adjustments, so the same player can have different numbers. Do not mix thresholds from one source with data from another.

How does StrokesEdge use strokes gained?

The model blends a long-run strokes gained baseline with recent form, then weights approach, off the tee, around the green and putting by how much each one has mattered at that specific course.
