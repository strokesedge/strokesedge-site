- **SG: Off the Tee — 13%.** Wide corridors let elite ball-strikers off the tee gain an edge without accuracy penalty.
- **Course-Fit Approach Component — 10%.** DG's course-fit approach component captures shot-shape/yardage-bucket fit specific to this layout's approach profile.
- **Driving Distance Fit — 8%.** Multiple reachable par-5s reward bombers who can attack scoring zones, but aggressive lines into prairie/water temper how heavily pure distance should be weighted relative to accuracy.
- **Greens in Regulation % — 8%.** A green-light, low-risk setup means GIR volume converts directly into birdie chances.
- **Driving Accuracy Fit — 7%.** Corridors are wide and the 3-inch rough itself is a minor impediment, but the real miss penalty here is severe: native prairie grass lines many fairways with a genuine lost-ball risk, and water is in play on more than half the holes. TPC Twin Cities has one of the highest missed-fairway rates on Tour despite its width — this factor is weighted for that hazard density, not just corridor width.
- **SG: Around the Green — 6%.** Missed-green recovery still matters around slick, subtly contoured surrounds.
- **Course-Fit Short-Game Component — 5%.** Short-game course-fit component adds granularity on chip/pitch demands specific to this green complex.
- **Birdie or Better % — 5%.** Birdie frequency is the literal currency of this event given the low cut lines and winning totals.
- **Scrambling % — up-and-down rate from missed greens — 3%.** Occasional missed greens still need saving, but low rough/penalty risk (as opposed to prairie/water risk, already captured by driving accuracy fit) caps its importance.

**Winner DNA gates this week:**
- SG: Approach >= -0.05. Approach play is the top differentiator; players with negative DG approach form rarely contend in this shootout.
- SG: Putting >= -0.1. Given how much winning scores depend on made putts, weak putters on the DG scale should be filtered out.
- Driving Distance Fit >= -0.15. Severely short-off-the-tee profiles struggle to access the scoring par-5s and short-iron look this course rewards.
- Greens in Regulation % >= 60. With wide fairways and low rough, any plausible winner should be hitting a clear majority of greens.
